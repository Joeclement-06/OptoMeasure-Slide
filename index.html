⚡ Quick Start
  1. Open VR View on patient's phone — note the 6-char room code.
  2. Open Controller on doctor's phone — enter code → Connect.
  3. Place patient's phone in cardboard. Use H-Offset until fused + appears at 0Δ.
  4. Select mode (BO or BI). Slide prism slowly upward.
  5. Record Blur, Break, Recovery at the appropriate prism.
